Application Process

A Simple and Professional Way to Apply

01

Review Opportunities

Review any vacancy published on this page or submit an expression of interest for a relevant future opportunity.

02

Prepare Your CV

Ensure your CV is current and clearly presents your relevant education, experience, strengths and professional skills. A short cover letter may also be included where relevant.

03

Submit Your Application

Send your CV through the official email address or WhatsApp number listed on this page. Please mention the position you are applying for. For a future expression of interest, mention the type of role you would like to be considered for.

04

Initial Review

Applications may be reviewed according to relevant skills, experience, role suitability and current business requirements. Submitting an application does not guarantee that every applicant will receive an individual response.

05

Interview

Shortlisted applicants may be invited to an online or in-person interview, depending on the role and current working arrangements. The interview may cover the applicant’s background, communication skills, availability, experience and suitability for the position.

06

Selection

A successful applicant will receive written confirmation of the offered role, primary responsibilities, working arrangement and applicable terms before starting work.

Fair Consideration

Applications are considered according to relevant qualifications, skills, experience, suitability and legitimate business requirements. We value respectful and fair consideration of applicants and do not knowingly make recruitment decisions based on irrelevant personal characteristics. Providing false, misleading or unauthorised information may result in an application being rejected or a recruitment process being discontinued.

No Recruitment Fee

CV & Cover Letter Nepal does not charge applicants a fee to apply for employment opportunities. Applicants should not send money, bank PINs, complete payment-card details, mobile banking credentials, passwords or OTP codes as part of a job application. Any suspicious recruitment communication should be verified using the official contact details published on this website.

Applicant Information and Privacy

Applicant CVs and other recruitment information will be used only to:

  • Review suitability for a current or relevant future opportunity.
  • Communicate regarding the recruitment process.
  • Conduct interviews or reasonable recruitment checks.
  • Maintain necessary recruitment records.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Applicants should provide only information relevant to employment consideration. Passport copies, bank details and other unnecessary sensitive documents should not be included in an initial application.

If an applicant asks to be considered for future opportunities, their CV and application information may be retained for up to six months with their permission. After that period, the information should normally be deleted unless:

  • The applicant agrees to a longer period.
  • An active recruitment process is continuing.
  • Retention is reasonably required by law or for dispute resolution.

Applicants may request correction or deletion of information held directly by us by contacting the email address or WhatsApp number listed below. Applicant information will not be publicly displayed, used as promotional content or shared for unrelated purposes without appropriate permission or legal authority. Additional information is available in our Privacy Policy.

Important Recruitment Information

  • Publishing a vacancy does not guarantee that a position will be filled.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee a response or interview.
  • Attending an interview does not guarantee an employment offer.
  • Position availability, duties and working arrangements may change according to legitimate business requirements.
  • Only written confirmation from CV & Cover Letter Nepal should be treated as an official employment offer.
  • Applicants remain responsible for ensuring that all submitted information is genuine and accurate.
