Our Story

Clear and Practical Support for Applicants

CV & Cover Letter Nepal was founded by Yubraj Timsina with a clear purpose: to make professional document preparation and online application support simple, accessible and reliable.

Many applicants struggle with document structure, formatting, online forms and changing application requirements. This service was created to provide clear, personalised and practical assistance throughout the process.

We support job seekers, students, professionals and other applicants by helping them prepare documents, organise genuine information and complete selected online application processes with greater clarity and confidence.

“I started CV & Cover Letter Nepal to help job seekers, students and other applicants prepare professional documents and complete online application processes with greater clarity and confidence. My focus is to understand each client’s actual requirements, explain the service scope clearly and provide personalised, practical support.”

Our Working Principles

Trusted Support Built Around Clarity and Care

Personalised Support

Each requirement is reviewed with attention to the client’s actual purpose, background and document needs.

Clear Communication

The service scope, quotation, next steps, required information and expected timeframe are explained before work begins.

Confidential Handling

Client information and supporting documents are treated as sensitive and used only for the agreed service, necessary communication, record-keeping or another authorised or legally required purpose.

Transparent Quotations

Pricing expectations, included deliverables and applicable payment requirements are communicated clearly before a service is confirmed.

Genuine Information

Documents and applications are prepared using genuine information provided or approved by the client. We do not knowingly assist with false, forged or misleading information.

Responsible Assistance

We provide independent document preparation and application support while encouraging clients to review official requirements and verify all factual information before submission.
