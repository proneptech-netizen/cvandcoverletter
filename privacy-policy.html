Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

At CV and Cover Letter Nepal ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible from cvandcoverletternepal.com, we understand the sensitivity of your personal and professional information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our document preparation services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when requesting our services. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and location.
  • Professional Information: Work history, educational background, skills, certifications, and career achievements.
  • Sensitive Documents: Depending on the service, this may include scanned copies of passports, citizenship certificates, academic transcripts, or government IDs strictly for application purposes (e.g., Passport, PAN, Labour Permit).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To draft, format, and deliver your requested documents (CVs, SOPs, Cover Letters, etc.).
  • To process online applications on your behalf (Government portals, EPS, Lok Sewa, Visas).
  • To communicate with you regarding your order, provide updates, and offer customer support.
  • To process payments and maintain financial records.

3. Data Confidentiality & Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your documents are treated with strict confidentiality. We implement industry-standard security measures, including restricted access and secure communication channels (like WhatsApp end-to-end encryption), to protect your data. Once your document is delivered and the service is complete, you may request that we permanently delete your sensitive files from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

To fulfill your requests, we may need to interact with third-party government portals (e.g., Nepal Passport Department, EPS-TOPIK portals, Lok Sewa Aayog) or booking systems (Flight/Hotel providers). We only submit the exact information you have authorized us to share for the explicit purpose of completing your application.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections if any information is inaccurate.
  • Request deletion of your personal data after the completion of services.
